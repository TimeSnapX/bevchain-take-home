How to read this

  • Weekly take-home is what a regular weekly payslip should look like after PAYG withholding. It is the useful “what hits my account” number.
  • Yearly salary is hours × rate × weeks worked. Yearly take-home then subtracts income tax, Medicare levy, and optional HELP / Medicare levy surcharge.
  • Super (12%) is paid by the employer on ordinary time earnings. It is not take-home cash. If overtime is switched on, super is only counted on the first 38 hours.
  • Casuals do not get paid annual leave. 52 weeks assumes you work every week. Drop weeks worked to 48 if you take four unpaid weeks off.
  • Overtime is an estimate using Storage Services-style loadings: ordinary casual rate to 38 hours, then 175% / 225% of the unloaded base. Confirm your actual BevChain / labour-hire agreement.
  • Medicare levy surcharge (1%+) can apply from $105,001 a year if you do not have private hospital cover. That is why 50 and 55 hour years can look a little different with the cover box unchecked.

Planning estimate only, not tax advice and not a payslip. Rates current for the 2026–27 Australian resident year. Shift penalties, allowances, public holidays, and salary packaging are not included.
